I have an old phone that has 70% (or maybe less) battery health. It was draining really quickly, especially with the last few OS updates and when I used mobile data instead of wifi. I had to bring charger everywhere and worry that my phone might dies at any time. Plus its getting super hot. I tried some tips on internet so you know what works (for me, and perhaps for you too)

Lets say I charged my phone to 96 in the morning. At 11 am after I opened some WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, and some other random apps, its gonna be left at around 79-67%. Thats a lot of drop. It was fine to me since I bring my cable with me anyways so I can always charge it. However, at this particular time, when I touch my phone it was super hot, like its burning, and it does not feel comfortable at all. The screen and apps were laggy too (probably because its hot so the performance throttled too). I dont have a problem if the battery run out quickly, what I dont want is that it drowns quickly and unusable. 

So I did some digging. Basically we all suspect that OS updates often make older version phone lag or have some issues. For awhile I did not notice that. I set the update on my phone to manual so I can decide what to install and what not. After a few pop ups saying I can update my thing, I figured maybe I have to, for security and support sake. So I did install this new OS that introduced the entire graphical magic thing they claim were beautiful. In truth, I dont really see it. That was probably what triggered the weird behavior on my phone. Often times the animation were laggy. My phone gets super hot and it becomes very laggy, practically unusable unless it cools down for a 10-30 minute break. 

I saw some posts on the forum. People have had success by reducing the transparency and animation for this OS update. Naturally I follow that. To my surprise, it works, somehow. Sure, the animation was gone and now my phone feels different (I havent gotten used to it yet). The transparency however, I did not notice anything different and I feel fine without it. It helps my phone stays a bit cooler than usual, which was very important as now I can use it without having to deal with noticeable lag. So that one fix alone helps. 

But guess what? I did more than just one fix. I was able to preserve my phone battery even longer by simply ....... not using it. Seriously, what do I need this brick for if not just to text my friends and family and occasionally checks on my emails. I have another device I can use for entertainment so my phone just holds my WhatsApp pretty much. Social medias? occasionally I would open Instagram to see funny reels but hey, that one time I got hooked up to its content and I couldnt stop. These doom scrolling thing is pretty much real and I was unconscious about it at that time. But now that I track what I have done in a day (probly will make a post about it later) I realized that doom scrolling occupy a major part of my day that I could have used for something more important. 

So I just ..... dont use my phone. Or at least I tried to. I deleted every apps on my homescreen and only left the most essentials ones. You can check what is essential to you via screen time. Just see what you used the most during the week, thats your essentials. But social medias would be one of them since we used them often, right? Sure, then you have to remove them somehow. What did I do with those apps? I make them hidden! (all phones should have this feature) I dont want to uninstall the app in case theres something coming up. But by putting them away where I cant physically sees and access them puts another layer of protection to me. I am not unconsciously open the app by remembering its location on screen and how many tap I have to do to get there, now if I really need to open it, I have to do series of actions longer than before. Also just simply not seeing it can save my mental state as if I dont have the apps so I focused on other things. 

So my tips: reduce the animations and transparency, hide the app that makes you doom scroll, and use your phone less. My phone now lasts an entire day, at least the morning, during my job, and after I went home. At 7pm I can still have 60% or more. Mind you my battery health was only 70% lol. So that 60% was not a lot but its still considerable enough. 

Why go such lengths and not buy new phone? Phone prices are crazy right now and I aint gonna spend millions right now. Imma try to survive lads. 

Hope this helps. Cheers!
